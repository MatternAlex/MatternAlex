# Hi

I'm a young developer building mostly iOS apps. The ideas for them usually come from books and movies, and I pick them out myself, without any AI. Then I turn them into apps, formulas and algorithms.

## The origin of my ideas
I read a lot, and while I read I look for ideas that nobody has built yet.
- **GFP** came from *Atomic Habits*. The book rates basketball players with one number, and every season a player has to improve it by about 1 percent. I wondered why fitness has no such number, one that shows how much better your whole fitness got. So I am building the formula for it.
- **LearnCenter** came from my own room. I was learning, but the household was waiting: cleaning, tidying. I like audiobooks, so I asked myself what if I could learn and tidy at the same time. That became cards that are read aloud, with answers you say out loud.

## What I'm building

| Project | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **[LearnCenter](https://github.com/MatternAlex/learncenter)** | A study app you can use without looking at a screen: flashcards with spaced repetition, an exam study planner, grades and a community | TestFlight beta |
| **[GFP](https://github.com/MatternAlex/gfp)** | One fitness score across calisthenics, strength and endurance, so progress becomes measurable | Web prototype done, native iOS app in progress |

## A problem I'm working on
Flashcard apps keep your eyes on the screen. In LearnCenter, your cards can be read aloud and you can answer out loud, so you can study on a walk, on the bus, or whenever you can't look at a screen.
